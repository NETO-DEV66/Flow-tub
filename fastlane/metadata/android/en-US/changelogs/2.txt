- First Flow Tube release: privacy-first YouTube and Music client for Android
- On-device recommendations that learn from what you watch, with nothing sent to a server
- Videos, Shorts and music with background playback, picture-in-picture and a queue you control
- Playlists, likes, Watch later, saved Shorts and downloads, all stored on the device
- Import and export your data, and pair two devices to sync over your own network
- SponsorBlock, quality and codec preferences, an equalizer and lyrics
- Material 3 themes, a true-black AMOLED style, home-screen widgets and a choice of launcher icons
- In-app updates from this project's own releases
