YouTube, without the tracking

A privacy-first YouTube and YouTube Music client for Android, with a recommendation engine that runs entirely on your phone. No account, no analytics, no data leaves your device.

What you get

On-device recommendations

Learns from what you watch, skip, and like — entirely offline. Inspect it, adjust it, or wipe it.

Video that behaves

Resolution switching, SponsorBlock, DeArrow, background play, PiP, casting, gestures, and downloads.

A real music player

Album art, queue control, shuffle and repeat, synced lyrics, and a persistent mini player.

Local library

Watch history, favourites, playlists, a Shorts feed, and cached subscription feeds.

Private by default

No Google account, no trackers. Import from NewPipe, export or delete everything anytime.

Made to look good

Eleven themes, Material 3 throughout, a home-screen widget, and a TV interface.

Build from source

You need JDK 17+ and the Android SDK (API 37).

git clone https://github.com/NETO-DEV66/Flow-tub.git
cd Flow-tub

# Debug build
./gradlew :app:assembleGithubDebug

# Release build — the single universal APK
./gradlew :app:assembleGithubRelease

Output lands in app/build/outputs/apk/github/release/.
