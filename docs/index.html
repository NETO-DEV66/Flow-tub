01

Set up — no account, no login

Flow Tube never asks you to sign in. There is no Google login, no password, and no cookie to hand over — so nothing about your Google account is ever at risk. Instead, you export a copy of your own YouTube data and import the file. It takes about two minutes.

0
logins, passwords or cookies ever requested
2 min
to export your data and import it
100%
of the import happens on your device
1

Ask Google for your data

Open takeout.google.com in any browser and sign in with the Google account whose YouTube data you want.

  • Click Deselect all — Takeout ticks every Google product by default.
  • Tick YouTube and YouTube Music — this carries your subscriptions, playlists and watch history.
  • Tick My Activity as well — this is where your liked videos and search history live. Skip it and likes will be missing.
  • Click Next step, leave the defaults (email link, .zip, 2 GB) and click Create export.
Open Google Takeout
2

Wait, then download the ZIP

Google builds the archive in the background — minutes to a few hours depending on how much history you have. You get an email titled “Your Google data is ready to download” with a Download button, or you can return to Takeout → Manage exports → Download.

  • A large export arrives as several ZIP parts. Download all of them.
  • Keep them together — Flow Tube reads every part of one export.
3

Import it into Flow Tube

  • Open Flow Tube → Settings → Backup & restore → Import.
  • Tap Import from Google Takeout.
  • Select every ZIP from your export and let it run. The progress line names each part as it lands: Subscriptions, Watch history, Likes and searches, Playlists, YouTube Music library.

When it finishes you get a summary of exactly what came in:

# example result
412 subscriptions · 8,930 history entries · 1,204 likes · 6 playlists

Your data never leaves your phone. The export is read on your device and written straight into the app's private database. Flow Tube has no server, no analytics and no upload of any kind.

Delete the ZIP afterwards. It holds your entire watch and search history — the most personal file on your phone. Once the import is done, remove it. Re-importing later is safe and never duplicates anything.

02

What you get

A full-featured client built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and Media3 — not a web wrapper.

On-device recommendations

Learns from what you watch, skip, like and search for — entirely offline. Inspect it, adjust it, or wipe it.

  • Weekday vs weekend, morning vs night patterns
  • Detects topic boredom and mixes in new content
  • Transparency dashboard — see why something was recommended

Video that behaves

  • Resolution switching up to 1080p
  • SponsorBlock — skips sponsors, intros, outros, filler
  • DeArrow — replaces clickbait thumbnails and titles
  • Return YouTube Dislike
  • Background play and Picture-in-Picture
  • Casting to smart TVs and streaming sticks
  • Gesture controls for brightness, volume and seeking
  • Downloads with VP9, AV1 and standard formats

A real music player

  • Album art and audio visualisations
  • Queue management with add, remove, reorder
  • Shuffle and repeat
  • Synchronised lyrics
  • Persistent mini player across the app

Library that stays local

  • Watch history, favourites and custom playlists
  • Shorts feed with bookmarking
  • Continue-watching shelf
  • Subscription management with cached feeds

Private by default

  • No Google account, login or cookie
  • No ads, analytics or tracking SDKs
  • All data in app-private local storage
  • Bring your own data in from Google Takeout
  • Export or delete everything at any time

Made to look good

  • 11 themes, including OLED black
  • Material 3 throughout
  • Home-screen now-playing widget
  • Adaptive navigation rail and a TV interface

Download

Every release ships one universal APK with both 32-bit and 64-bit ARM code, so there is no ABI to choose. The app also updates itself — it checks for a new release and installs it after you confirm.

Get the latest build

Signed with the same key every time, so a new release installs straight over the old one without losing your data.

Download APK All releases

FlowTube-vX.Y.Z-universal.apk · ~23 MB · Android 8.0+

Verify what you downloaded

Each release publishes a checksums.txt next to the APK.

sha256sum -c checksums.txt

apksigner verify --print-certs \
  FlowTube-vX.Y.Z-universal.apk

05

Build from source

You need JDK 21 and the Android SDK (platform android-37.0, build-tools 37.0.0). One product flavour exists: github — always use flavour-prefixed tasks.

Compile it

git clone https://github.com/NETO-DEV66/Flow-tub.git
cd Flow-tub

# debug
./gradlew :app:assembleGithubDebug

# release — the universal APK
./gradlew :app:assembleGithubRelease

Output lands in app/build/outputs/apk/github/release/.

Under the hood

  • Kotlin with Jetpack Compose and Material 3
  • Hilt for dependency injection
  • Media3 / ExoPlayer for playback
  • Room and DataStore for local data
  • InnerTube client with a NewPipeExtractor fallback
  • Glance for the home-screen widget
