NEUROTUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.5.0
DATE: 2026-10-21
STATUS: RELEASE

NEW
- Import your account's library. On Settings > Account, with a session signed in, tap Import library and the app reads what the account holds
- Subscriptions: the channels your account follows are brought in, a page at a time, and appear in the Subscriptions feed like any you added by hand
- Artists: the artists your account follows on YouTube Music are added as music subscriptions
- Liked songs: the songs your account liked land in Liked music, newest first
- Playlists and albums: the playlists and albums your account saved are stored with their songs, so they open and play from the library
- The row names the part it is on while it runs, and lists how many of each kind were added when it finishes

NOTES
- Importing only reads. Nothing is written back to the account and nothing is deleted
- Re-importing is safe: anything already present is left as it is
- Each part is tried on its own, so one failing does not lose the others
- The session is still a cookie, stored encrypted on the device only. It never leaves the phone except in requests to YouTube itself
- One universal APK for every supported device, as before
- Same signing key as every earlier release
