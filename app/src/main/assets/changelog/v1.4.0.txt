NEUROTUBE CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 1.4.0
DATE: 2026-10-20
STATUS: RELEASE

NEW
- Sign in to YouTube. Open Settings > Account, or use the card in onboarding, and a Google sign-in page opens inside the app
- Once signed in, browse and search use your account: subscriptions, liked videos and playlists come back personalised instead of anonymous
- The account name is shown on the settings row and on the Account page, and you can sign out at any time
- Signing in is optional. Everything works signed out, exactly as before

NOTES
- The session is a cookie, stored encrypted on the device only. It never leaves the phone except in requests to YouTube itself
- Sign-out clears both the in-memory session and the saved copy on disk
- The account page and the onboarding card can both be skipped without any effect on the rest of the app
- One universal APK for every supported device, as before
- Same signing key as every earlier release
